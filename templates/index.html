How it works

  1. Extract. We pull the text out of your PDF with pypdf.
  2. Speak. Google Translate TTS turns the text into natural-sounding speech.
  3. Listen. You download a single MP3. Play it anywhere.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. The conversion runs on free TTS endpoints. If it saves you time, the coffee button keeps the lights on.

Does it work on scanned PDFs?

Not yet — scanned pages have no extractable text. OCR support is on the roadmap.

Do you store my files?

No. Files live in a temp folder during conversion and are wiped on server restart.
